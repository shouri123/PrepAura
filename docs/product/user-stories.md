# User Stories & Personas: PrepAura

## Target Personas

### Persona 1: Elena - Staff Systems Architect

- **Background**: 10+ years in backend engineering; preparing for L6/E6 loops at tier-1 distributed infrastructure companies.
- **Pain Point**: Current platforms offer trivial tree inversion problems rather than high-stakes distributed architecture trade-offs.
- **Needs**: High-rigor prompts with ambiguous failure modes, strict time constraints, and multi-vector scoring.

### Persona 2: Marcus - Engineering Leader / Bar Raiser

- **Background**: Engineering Director or Principal Architect calibrating evaluation rubrics for candidate loops.
- **Pain Point**: Lack of standardized scoring benchmarks across interview panels.
- **Needs**: Observable rubric criteria (Technical Rigor, Structured Delivery, Problem Decomposition, Edge-Case Conviction).

---

## User Stories

### Epics & Acceptance Criteria

#### US-101: Chamber Parameter Tuning

- **As a** candidate preparing for a Staff role,
- **I want to** select my target track (Distributed Systems) and rigor tier (Stress-Test),
- **So that** the simulation tests my ability to handle production scale and ambiguous failure modes.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - Selection of track, seniority, rigor, and demeanor must persist in state.
  - Microphone stream must be validated prior to chamber entry.

#### US-102: Real-Time Waveform & Audio Telemetry

- **As a** candidate speaking through an architectural design,
- **I want to** see live audio waveform telemetry and a synchronized timer,
- **So that** I maintain concise pacing and structured delivery.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - Web Audio API canvas visualizer must animate in real-time.
  - Timer must count down accurately without drift.

#### US-103: Multi-Vector Assessment Debrief

- **As a** candidate who has completed an interview session,
- **I want to** receive an automated quantitative and qualitative debrief,
- **So that** I can identify specific gaps in my distributed systems knowledge.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - Overall conviction score must be calculated as a weighted average `[0, 100]`.
  - Four competency vectors must be presented with benchmark model answers.

#### US-104: Longitudinal Remediation Tracking

- **As a** candidate practicing across multiple weeks,
- **I want to** view my historical score trajectories and a 7-day remediation regimen,
- **So that** I systematically eliminate recurring architectural blindspots.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - Historical sessions must be plotted on a longitudinal Recharts area chart.
  - Automated weakness analysis must recommend concrete technical study topics.
